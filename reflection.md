# Reflection Comparisons

- High-Energy Pop vs Chill Lofi: Pop profile pushes high-energy upbeat tracks to the top, while Chill Lofi prefers low-energy chill songs; this makes sense because energy similarity and mood matching shift in opposite directions.
- High-Energy Pop vs Deep Intense Rock: Both prefer energetic songs, but genre matching separates them; pop-genre songs stay high for pop users while intense rock songs rise for rock users.
- Chill Lofi vs Deep Intense Rock: These are almost opposite preferences, so results diverge strongly; lofi profile rewards calm acoustic tracks, while rock profile favors intense high-energy tracks.

In plain language: songs like `Gym Hero` keep appearing for upbeat profiles because the scoring recipe strongly rewards high energy plus pop genre alignment. When a profile asks for calm moods and lower energy, those songs lose points quickly.
